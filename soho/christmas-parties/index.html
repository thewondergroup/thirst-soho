The boring questions, answered

Is there a hire fee?

No. There is no hire fee for private hire or a reserved section. We take a deposit to hold the date, which comes off your final bill.

How many people can you hold?

Upstairs holds 70 standing and downstairs holds 80. Exclusive hire of both floors is 150. Smaller groups can book a table, a booth or a section.

Do you do drinks packages?

Yes. Pre-booked parties can choose a package with a set price per head. See the Drinks menu for details, or ask and we'll tailor one.

Can we eat?

We serve grazing nibbles upstairs and guests can order food to the table from Suvlaki next door. Non-alcoholic cocktails are on the menu for anyone not drinking.

What if numbers change?

Bookings of up to 20 need 24 hours' notice to cancel and larger groups need 72 hours. With less notice the deposit is kept. Keep us updated and we'll adjust your space where we can.

Is the venue accessible?

Our building is old and full of character, which means the toilets are downstairs and not wheelchair accessible. Let your team know in advance.
